The Problem

India's rural healthcare crisis
demands urgent innovation

Over 65% of India's population lives in rural areas, yet they receive less than 30% of healthcare services. The gap is widening — and preventable deaths are the price.

8.4L
Preventable Deaths Annually
Deaths from conditions like sepsis, cardiac events, and complicated pregnancies that could have been avoided with timely diagnosis and care.
62%
Delay in Seeking Care
Patients wait an average of 48 hours before seeking professional help — often arriving at hospitals when conditions have already worsened critically.
1:2000
Doctor-to-Patient Ratio
In rural India, one doctor serves 2,000+ patients on average — compared to WHO's recommended 1:1000. Telemedicine is the only scalable bridge.
How It Works

From symptom to care,
in under 10 minutes

AarogyaConnect is a four-step intelligent pipeline that works even on low-bandwidth networks and in 12 regional languages.

Step 01
Multilingual Symptom Input
Patients describe symptoms via voice or text in Hindi, Tamil, Punjabi, Bengali, and 8 more languages. Works on basic smartphones with 2G connectivity.
Step 02
AI Triage & Risk Scoring
Our clinical NLP model assesses severity — critical, moderate, or stable — in under 3 seconds, referencing India's disease burden and seasonal outbreak patterns.
Step 03
Doctor Matching & Routing
Critical cases are instantly matched to the nearest available specialist for video/audio teleconsult. Non-urgent cases get self-care guidance and nearby PHC directions.
Step 04
Follow-up & Monitoring
Automated SMS/WhatsApp reminders for medicines, follow-up appointments, and vaccination schedules. Families get health summaries in local languages.
Core Features

Built for the realities
of rural India

AI Symptom Triage (12 languages)
Clinical-grade NLP trained on India's disease burden data. Distinguishes fever from malaria from dengue — contextually, locally.
Telemedicine with Audio Fallback
Video consultations for 4G areas; degrades gracefully to voice-only on 2G. Doctors can prescribe digitally via a verifiable token.
Offline Emergency Routing
Cached maps of nearest PHCs, hospitals, and blood banks. Works without internet. Sends SMS alerts to nearest emergency contact on critical triage.
Medicine Reminders & Adherence
SMS and WhatsApp reminders for chronic disease patients — diabetes, hypertension, TB — with caregiver alerts if doses are missed for 2+ days.
Public Health Dashboard
District-level outbreak detection. Health officials see real-time symptom clusters, anomalies, and can deploy rapid response teams in 1 click.
